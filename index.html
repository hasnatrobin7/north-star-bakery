Welcome to North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery for weekday commuters, families, and anyone planning a small gathering. We bake bread, pastries, and cakes by hand each morning, and we welcome pre orders and questions.

What We're Known For

Neighbors stop in for the seeded country loaf, butter-layered morning pastries, and simple celebration cakes. Nothing is mass produced. If you need something for a meeting or a family table, we can set it aside for pickup.
