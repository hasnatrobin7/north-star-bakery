    Breads

    Country loaves, sandwich bread, and rolls are baked before we open. Most loaves cost $6 to $12. The Signature Loaf is the one neighbors ask for by name.

    Pastries

    Morning buns, cookies, and other small pastries are sold by the piece, usually $3 to $6. The case is fullest early in the day and thins out after lunch.

    Cakes

    Celebration cakes are made to order for birthdays and small gatherings. Prices usually fall between $28 and $60, depending on size. Tell us the date, the size, and any allergy notes when you write.

    Pricing

    These are counter prices, not an online store. We do not take payment on this website. Ask at the counter, or send a pre order request and we will confirm what we can hold for you.
