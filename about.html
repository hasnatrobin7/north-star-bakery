About the Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small counter for neighbors who wanted bread that still tasted like it came from someone's kitchen. We are still that shop: a warm, handmade bakery in the middle of the community, open for commuters in the morning and families later in the day.

How We Source Ingredients

We keep the ingredient list short. Flour, butter, and seasonal fruit come from suppliers we can name, and we choose them because they suit a neighborhood bakery rather than a factory line. If a loaf needs less, we do not add more.

The People at the Counter

The morning bakers finish the loaves before the door opens. The counter team helps with pastry choices, cake questions, and pre orders for event planners. If you are not sure what to pick up, ask. That conversation is part of the shop.
